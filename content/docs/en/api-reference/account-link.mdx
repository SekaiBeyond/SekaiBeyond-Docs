---
title: The AccountLink object
description: Your app's user and the sekaibeyond.com account they're bound to.
---

What [Create a binding](./create-account-link.mdx) and [Get a binding](./get-account-link.mdx) return.

A binding pairs one of your app's users with one sekaibeyond.com account. Each side is bound at most once. See [Binding accounts](../ticket-api/account-links.mdx).

## Fields

<Params>
<Param name="appUserId" type="string">
Your app's ID for the user, as sent when binding.
</Param>
<Param name="uid" type="string">
The account's ID on the site. It never changes, so it's what to store.
</Param>
<Param name="displayName" type="string">
The account's name on the site, as it is now.
</Param>
<Param name="photoURL" type="string">
The account's photo as it is now, or `""`.
</Param>
<Param name="nickname" type="string">
What the user was called in your app when they bound, as sent.
</Param>
<Param name="linkedAt" type="string">
When they bound.
</Param>
</Params>
