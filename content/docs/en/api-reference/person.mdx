---
title: The Person object
description: A person's account here, without their email.
---

What [Get the signed-in person](./get-me.mdx), [List the team](./list-team.mdx) and [List an event's staff](./list-event-staff.mdx) return.

It never includes the person's email.

## Fields

<Params>
<Param name="uid" type="string">
The person's account ID on the site, and the `uid` of their Firebase user in a registered app's project.
</Param>
<Param name="displayName" type="string">
Their display name.
</Param>
<Param name="photoURL" type="string">
URL of their profile photo, or `""` for none.
</Param>
<Param name="group" type="string">
Their group on the site: `president`, `core-staff`, `staff` or `user`.
</Param>
<Param name="title" type="string">
Their English title on the team page, or `""`.
</Param>
<Param name="titleCn" type="string">
Their Chinese title on the team page, or `""`.
</Param>
</Params>
